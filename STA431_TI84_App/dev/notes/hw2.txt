1. Let A, B, and C be three events in a sample space. Write set-theoretic expressions (using
unions, intersections, and complements) for the events that:
(a) only A occurs,
(b) exactly one of the three events occurs,
(c) at least one event occurs,
(d) A and B occur, but not C.
2. Given P(X) = 0.55, P(Y ) = 0.35, and P(X ∪Y ) = 0.70. Find:
(a) P(X ∩Y )

3. In a university department of 120 individuals, there are 70 graduate students and 50 faculty
members. Among the graduate students, 40 identify as female. In total, there are 65 females
in the department. If an individual is selected at random:
(a) What is the probability the selected individual is a male faculty member?
(b) If the selected individual is female, what is the probability she is a graduate student?
(Hint: Construct a 2 × 2 contingency table to organize the joint probabilities).

(b) If a paper is accepted for publication, what is the probability that it was a Methodology
paper?
